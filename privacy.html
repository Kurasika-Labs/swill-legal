Swill – Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

Swill is a wine-tasting bluffing game for iOS and Android made by Kurasika Technologies Limited (company number 17067978, registered office 12 Old Mills Industrial Estate, Paulton, Bristol, BS39 7SU, United Kingdom). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Questions and requests: support@kurasika.tech.

2. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase – hosts our database and file storage in the EU (Ireland).
  • OpenAI – draws the portrait from your photo, and polishes bluff text when you ask for it. Nothing is used to train their models.
  • WorkOS – handles Sign in with Apple and Google.
  • RevenueCat – records in-app purchases.
  • Apple and Google – app distribution and payment.

Each of these acts on our instructions under a data-processing agreement. Where data leaves the UK or EU (OpenAI, WorkOS and RevenueCat are in the United States) the transfer is covered by standard contractual clauses.

3. Legal basis

We process your data to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract) and, for the optional portrait and sign-in features, on the basis of your consent, which you give by using the feature and can withdraw by deleting the portrait or your account.

4. How long we keep it

Your profile and game history are kept while your account exists. Uploaded photos are deleted immediately after the drawing is made. If you delete your account, everything tied to it is removed within 30 days; anonymised game statistics that cannot identify you may be kept.

5. Deleting your account

Open Profile in the app and tap Delete my account. This removes your profile, portrait, notes, invite record and purchase record from our systems. You can also email support@kurasika.tech from any address and we will delete the account within 30 days. Purchases are refunded, if at all, by Apple or Google under their own rules.

6. Your rights

Under UK and EU data-protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict processing, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local authority. Write to support@kurasika.tech.

7. Children

Swill is a game about wine and is intended for adults of legal drinking age. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
