Delete your Swill account

Swill is made by Kurasika Technologies Limited. You can delete your account and the data tied to it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Swill and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is removed immediately and cannot be restored.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email support@kurasika.tech with the subject "Delete my Swill account" and the display name you used. If you signed in with Apple or Google, send the email from that address so we can match the account. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, display name and portrait
  • Any photo used to draw the portrait (these are already deleted as soon as the drawing is made)
  • Your tasting notes, bluffs and votes
  • Your invite code and any record of who invited you
  • Your Swill Pro purchase record on our side (Apple or Google keep their own purchase history)

Anonymised game statistics that cannot identify you may be kept. Nothing else is retained.
